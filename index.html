NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I personalise my gift?

Yes. You can enter a recipient name, write a personal message, upload photos and choose gift details.

Can I upload my own music?

Yes. You can upload an audio file and play it in the current browser session.

Can I preview my gift?

Yes. The Personal Gift Studio shows a live preview of your name and message.

Can I share my finished gift?

Online sharing is a planned feature. It needs online storage and a publishing system before it can work.

Are my uploaded photos stored online?

No. At the moment, photo and music previews are local to your current browser session.
