A few helpful answers

Questions, answered.

Can I see the gift before publishing?

Yes. The creation journey ends with a preview screen. You can go back and edit your choices before publishing.

Does one ₹99 payment create more than one gift?

No. The intended rule is one successful payment for one new Gift Card. A separate new card requires another payment.

Can I upload my own photos and music?

You can preview selected files in this browser. In this frontend-only version, they are not uploaded to an online server.

Will the share link work now?

Not yet. A real shareable gift needs online storage, a database and a publishing service.

Is the ₹99 payment live?

No. Payment is intentionally not simulated. A trusted payment provider and secure server-side verification must be connected first.
