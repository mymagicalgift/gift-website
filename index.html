Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful answers before you create your first magical gift.

What is My Magical Gift?

It is a website where you create a personal digital gift with your own photos, messages and surprises, and share it through a private link.

How do I create a gift?

Choose an occasion, add the details and write your message in the creator above. Your progress is saved on the device you are using, so you can come back and finish later.

Can I add personal photographs?

Yes. Your photos appear in a memory gallery inside the gift, with soft frames and smooth transitions.

Can I write my own message?

Yes. Write your own message, letter or wishes. It appears in the gift exactly as you wrote it.

Can I add a voice message?

Yes. A personal voice message can be part of the Words From the Heart experience.

Can I choose a gift theme?

Yes. Choose from four gift formats: a gift box, an envelope, a scroll and a treasure chest. Each format has several designs.

How much does one gift cost?

For the first 50 gifts in the launch offer, a gift costs ₹49 plus a ₹1 service fee, a total of ₹50. After that, a gift costs ₹99 plus a ₹1 service fee, a total of ₹100. The service fee is always shown before you pay.

What if my payment fails?

If a payment fails or is cancelled, no gift is created for it. If money was taken but no gift appeared, contact us with your payment details and we will check it.

Can I get a refund?

If a payment goes through but your gift cannot be created because of a problem on our side, contact us. We will either fix the problem or refund your payment.

Can I get free access?

Sometimes we offer limited free access with a special redeem code. Each code can be used by a limited number of people, and each person can use it only once.

Can I share my gift?

Yes. Every gift gets its own private link. Anyone with that link can open the gift, and if you add a PIN, they also need the PIN.

Does the recipient need an account?

No. The recipient opens the gift through your link and does not need to create an account.

Can I add background music?

Yes. Choose optional background music or upload your own. Some browsers ask the recipient to tap Play before the music starts.

Can the recipient interact with the gift?

Yes. They can open a 3D gift box, blow out birthday candles, play mini-games and pop balloon wishes.

Can I protect my gift with a PIN?

Yes. Add a 4-digit PIN and the recipient must enter it before the gift opens. Share the PIN separately from the link.

Is my privacy protected?

Your gift is shared only through its own private link. We collect only the details needed to create your gift and process your payment. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and how we protect it.

Is my gift stored securely?

We store your gift with access controls that limit who can see it. Payments are checked on secure servers, not in your browser.

Can I rate the website?

Yes. Visitors who have opened a gift can leave an honest star rating and a short review. Only real ratings are shown.
